# Sims 4 saves on a second PC: posts on X

Searched X on 24 Nov 2026 (keyword, semantic, thread reads). Every item is a post from that search. All of it is opinion. Nothing below is filled in from memory.

Post links are `https://x.com/<handle>/status/<id>`. Dates are GMT.

## What repeats

People describe a **one-way copy** onto a new machine, not two live copies of one save. The folders they name are under Documents, Electronic Arts, The Sims 4: **Saves, Tray, and Mods**. Forgetting Tray is the loss people warn about. Size (tens to hundreds of GB) is why they pick a USB stick, an external drive, or a cable between the two PCs.

**OneDrive sitting on Documents** is the failure that shows up again and again: files turned cloud-only, a save cut in half, an old save written back over a new one, or the folder deleted while trying to keep it out of the sync. Google Drive and Dropbox show up as a place to park a copy or send a zip, not as the folder the game runs from. No post about a home NAS came back. A search for "nas" hit unrelated words.

**Same EA or Origin account, two computers:** one 2020 post says packs followed the account from a PC to a Mac. Several posts say packs bought on a console did not appear on PC. Nobody in these results described two Windows PCs signed in at once. Nobody described a partner or a legacy played back and forth on two computers. The near misses are a shared childhood computer, a friend who used someone else's Origin login, and one person who says they run mods on both a PC and a laptop.

No post named a tool that syncs a save between two PCs. Tray Importer, a plain folder copy, and "back up Documents before a patch" are what people praise. Save corruption in these posts is mostly blamed on a game update, not on the copy. One person moved 100 GB of mods and then said five years of saves were corrupted. That is one report, not a pattern.
