# Missing Sims 4 mods on a second PC: posts on X

Searched X on 24 Nov 2026 (keyword, semantic, thread reads). Every item is a post from that search. All of it is opinion. Nothing below is filled in from memory.

Post links are `https://x.com/<handle>/status/<id>`. Dates are GMT.

## What repeats

Nobody in these results posted a Sims 4 CurseForge share code, profile export, or modpack and said it worked or failed. A search for `curseforge://` and for "Install with CurseForge" plus Sims returned no Sims 4 install link.

What people do post is a **folder copy**: Mods, often with Tray and saves, on a USB stick, an external drive, or a cloud upload. The failures they name are script mods left off, old or corrupt files from a cloud sync, and script mods breaking after a game patch. No post said the CurseForge app stopped tracking a copied Mods folder.

Creators who post about redistribution tell people not to re-host the files. Several name their own site, Patreon, and CurseForge as the allowed download. Early-access leaks and third-party mod managers are called out. One creator allowed CurseForge and still rejected another manager that listed their mods without consent.
