## Decisions already made (Fable's part, `updater.ts`; one test per sentence, with a fake engine)

- `createUpdater(input, seams?)` (`seams`: `engine?`, `busy?`, `now?`) wraps the 6.8.x `autoUpdater`, loaded with a default `import` (the lint forbids
  `require`). Before the first check it sets `autoDownload`, `autoInstallOnAppQuit`, `allowDowngrade` and
  `allowPrerelease` to `false`, `disableWebInstaller` and `disableDifferentialDownload` to `true`, `requestHeaders`
  to `{ 'x-user-staging-id': '0' }`, and a logger that drops everything.
- **The feed comes only from `app-update.yml` inside the installed app.** It never calls `setFeedURL`. No variable,
  setting or IPC value can point it elsewhere. `SIMS_HELPER_UPDATES=off` can only turn it off.
- `off` when the app is not packaged, not on Windows, switched off, or `installDir` is inside (or contains) a
  `keepOutOf` folder, any letter case. `--updated` in `argv` sets `justUpdated`. A lower or equal version is `idle`.
- `check()` during `checking`, `downloading` or `ready` returns the state unchanged and never downloads.
  `download()` works only from `available`; a checksum error is `failed` with `bad-file`. `install()` works only
  from `ready` and calls `quitAndInstall(true, true)` once. No state or thrown error holds raw error text.

## Decisions already made (builder's part)

- **Handlers** check every request's shape and log nothing. `update:check` with `by: 'start'` checks at most once
  per 24 hours; `by: 'click'` always checks. `update:install` answers `busy` while `ctx.busy()` is true.
- **`UpdateBanner`** calls `update:check` (`by: 'start'`) on mount and every 24 hours. It renders nothing for `off`,
  `idle`, `checking`, a failed check or a rejected call. While `downloading` it asks `update:get` once a second.
  **Try again** calls `update:check` (`click`), then `update:download`. **Later** hides it until the next start. Its
  texts: research file, section 4, word for word. Also: refused as `busy`, "Sims Helper is in the middle of a
  change. Wait for it to finish, then try again."; `justUpdated`, a toast "Sims Helper was updated."
- **`UpdateRow`** (About): "Version 0.2.0", **Check for updates**, then "You have the newest version." or "Couldn't
  check right now. Try again later." With updates `off`: the version and a short note saying why. No release notes from the network.
- **`scripts/release/stage.ts`** (plain Node, no new package): reads `files[0].url` and `sha512` from
  `<dist>/latest.yml` with regular expressions, copies the installer to `<out>/` **named exactly that url**,
  recomputes the SHA-512, fails on a mismatch, and copies `latest.yml` beside it.
- **`release.yml`:** the shape in the research file, section 3. Only the `publish` job holds `contents: write`, with
  no checkout and no npm. No stored secret. It leaves a draft, published by hand. Its proof is the one below, from
  the newest published release.
- **Tests first:** handlers (the 24-hour rule, `busy`, bad requests); `stage.ts` (the name, spaces, a mismatch
  fails); components with `fakeApi` (each text, Later, Try again, polling only while downloading, a rejected call).
